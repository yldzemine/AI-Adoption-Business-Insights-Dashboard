# 🤖 AI Adoption & Business Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes AI adoption across different industries using Power BI. It explores how artificial intelligence influences business performance, revenue growth, productivity, customer satisfaction, innovation, and risk management.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze AI adoption by industry
- Measure the impact of AI investments
- Evaluate productivity and revenue growth
- Compare customer satisfaction across sectors
- Examine AI risks and future trends

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard
- AI adoption by industry
- Revenue growth
- Productivity analysis
- Country comparison
- KPI cards

### 2. AI Investment & Performance Analysis
- AI budget by sector
- Customer satisfaction
- Cost reduction
- Employee satisfaction

### 3. AI Risks & Future Trends
- AI risk management
- Data privacy compliance
- Innovation score by region
- Ethical compliance

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
